# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme’s project management guidance for cross-functional projects, from validating an idea through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The documents provide a shared reference for teams to coordinate work, communicate progress, manage risk, and deliver customer value.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme begins by validating the business need, identifying stakeholders, defining measurable outcomes, and making a go/no-go decision. Approved initiatives move into planning, where teams define scope, prioritize and estimate backlog items, agree on milestones and a Definition of Done, and identify risks and dependencies.

During execution, teams deliver iteratively and track work on a project board. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, resources, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team check-ins, delivery updates, and milestone demos keep progress visible, while blockers are escalated through the agreed path.

Quality is built into delivery with unit and integration tests as appropriate, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, and CI checks for tests, linting, and security. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security scans, release notes, smoke-test readiness, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Teams verify deployments and communicate releases, then hold retrospectives and track owned improvement actions.
